# THE REVENUE AGENT — The Offer

_The one page. If the offer can't be said from this page, it's not ready. Everything else (proposals, outreach) spins out of this._

---

## THE ONE LINE

**I build your business one clever tool that earns its keep — then it runs itself.**

Not a website. Websites don't make money. I build the *thing on the website that does* — a tool that quotes, calculates, books, or chases, specific to your trade.

---

## WHAT IT IS (in plain English, for the prospect)

Most web people sell you a brochure — a nice page that sits there. I sell you **one working tool** that does a job you'd otherwise pay a person to do.

Example: for a butcher, a calculator that turns a whole carcass into an instant customer quote in ten seconds. Nobody's staff has to work it out on paper. It just runs.

Built once. Then it works while you sleep.

---

## THE PRICING (built for low-key, not for a treadmill)

| | Price | What it's FOR |
|---|---|---|
| **Build it** | **£1,000–£1,500 one-off** | The tool, built and installed. This is the real work, paid up front. |
| **Keep it running** | **£25–£39/mo** | Three things only: it stays live, I own the tech plumbing (hosting + the AI bits), and I fix it if it breaks. |

**The boundary that protects my time:** the monthly is NOT "ask me for anything." New features or changes = a separate small job, quoted on its own. This is the single rule that turns the monthly from a nag-magnet into money that shows up while I do nothing.

**Design rule that keeps my load near-zero:** the client edits their own data (prices, hours, products) in a simple sheet — they never email me for that. Kill the nagging at the design stage, not with willpower.

## THE TWO TIERS (don't sell both at once)

**Tier 1 — The Tool (the opener).** One on-page tool that does a money-job. Demo-able today with the calculator. This is what I lead with.

**Tier 2 — The Automation (the upsell, later).** Once they trust me: an automation that does a *staff* job — enquiry comes in → auto-reply, logged, chased so no lead goes cold. This is where the monthly becomes undeniable. Hold it back until Tier 1 has landed. Never front-load it.

_(This is the shrunk-down version of the "AI architecture for companies" model — I don't re-architect their company, I install one automation that does one job. The AI handyman for local business.)_
